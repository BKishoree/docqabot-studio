Scope: the whole DocQABot Studio SPA (workspace, conversation, library, status, not found). Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: analysts and operations leads checking answers against their own policies, contracts and handbooks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The answer is the summary and the highlighted passage is the evidence. This app refuses the chat-bubble AI look (gradient, sparkle, bubbles) and shows answers the way a reviewer marks up a photocopy.
OWN-WORLD: Cool photocopy-white work area (#f5f6f4), toner-black ink (#1a1c1e), a deep graphite rail (#23282b) with highlighter-yellow (#ffe45c) only for quoted evidence and the active nav mark. Red pencil (#b3261e) for errors and deletes, ballpoint blue (#1d4ed8 family, deepened to #1f47b8) for links and focus. Interface text in Public Sans, quoted document text in Source Serif 4. Hairline 1px rules, 6px radii, no cards inside cards.
STORY: The user sees what is indexed, asks a question, reads a short answer, and checks it against ranked, highlighted passages without leaving the screen. Simulated and degraded states are always stated.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Graphite rail on the left with Ask, Library, Status and the recent conversations. The work column is at most 760px wide, with the readiness line and the composer in it. In a thread, each answer is followed by a SOURCES list of numbered margin marks, and the passages are set in serif with the question's words highlighted.
FORM: Highlighted photocopy, candidate 7 of 7 on my grounded list; seed:f6845ef62429a18e. Signature move: passages under a highlighter mark, with margin rank numbers. Raises: hairline density from japanese-high-density-web; stepped, short motion (no floaty easing) from rw-manual-acetate-tab-board.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
